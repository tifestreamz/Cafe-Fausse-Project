About Us

OUR STORY

A Room Built for the Moment

Vault opened in a converted bank vault in 2012 with a simple ambition — to make fine dining feel personal again. Every dish leaves the kitchen the way it would be served to family: unhurried, deliberate, and generous.
OUR PHILOSOPHY

Close Sourcing, Long Aging, Quiet Confidence

We work with a handful of farms and fishmongers we've known for years, age our meats in-house, and change the menu with the seasons rather than the calendar. Nothing on the table is there by accident.
"Hospitality is the art of making people feel like they arrived somewhere they belong."
THE TEAM

Led From the Pass

Our head chef and a tight brigade of a dozen run the kitchen nightly, joined front-of-house by a team who know most regulars by name and every dish by heart.
